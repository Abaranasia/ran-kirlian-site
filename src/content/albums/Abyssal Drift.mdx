Abyssal Drift is a mesmerizing long-form sonic experience, spanning over 74 minutes, that aims for an introspective journey in the vastness of the void. Step into a boundless emptiness, where the known dissolves into shadows and invisible whispers, through veils of crimson fog. Abyssal Drift invites you on an immersive journey through fathomless depths, where time unravels, and all sense of direction is lost.

Ethereal drones and evolving atmospheres form the currents of this drifting soundscape, carrying the listener through the vast, unfathomable expanse of an abyss. The music of this album explores themes of solitude, mystery, and introspection, evoking the slow and timeless pull of unseen forces. Dive into the comfortable darkness of Abyssal Drift and watch from light years away as your fears, worries and regrets orbit a distant, warm sun. This is not merely an album; it is an experience—a descent into the unknown and a surrender to the slow, aimless motion of a cosmos untethered. Let yourself be carried by the sound, adrift in the endless abyss.
