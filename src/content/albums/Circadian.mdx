Thirteen years after their first collaboration and six years after their last, Spanish ambient artists Ran Kirlian and Landru join forces again for this dark, mellow sound experience partially recorded live between 2023 and 2024.

With Circadian, the duo craft a mesmerizing sonic journey through the ebb and flow of time, mirroring the natural rhythms that govern existence. This album, built upon intricate synthesizer textures, organic samples, and evocative field recordings, invites the listener into a realm where drone atmospheres dissolve into expansive soundworlds, and deep, shadowed spaces give way to moments of luminous transcendence. From the delicate emergence of Dawn of Possibilities to the mercurial transformations of Life in Reflections, and the enigmatic void of The Empty Partition, each piece unfolds like a phase of an unseen cycle. The ethereal Awakening of the Mist drifts into the towering resonance of Ascension Dome, marking a culmination of sonic elevation.

A meditation on time, transition, and the unseen forces that shape our world, Circadian is both an immersive soundscape and a reflection of the cyclical nature of existence. Let its currents pull you into the flow.
