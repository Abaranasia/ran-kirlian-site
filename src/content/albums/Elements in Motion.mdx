After 3 years of exploring new paths in the dark-ambient side of my music, this album collects tracks that I prepared slowly and deeply. I wanted to do my best on it, and close friends points it as my best album til now. I'm really proud about the final result and I'm just working in a new album in a similar way. Mostly dark and drone based, source material comes from field recordings, voice and found sounds. Synthesizers have here a minimal presence.
