Fourth instance in the series of recordings based in long format drones and processed raw material. This volume contains one large piece as a result of a single recording session and several evolving process during 2019. This track was originally created as an inspirational source for my own as well as for meditative purposes. This piece may sound a bit raw and unpredictible, which is part of the spirit of this recording series.
