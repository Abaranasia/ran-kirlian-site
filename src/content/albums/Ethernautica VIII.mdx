Eighth volume in the series of recordings based in long format drones and processed raw material. This volume contains three long-form pieces based on improvised recordings that have undergone various editing and time stretching processes between September and December 2023. These tracks were originally created for myself as a source of inspiration and for meditative purposes.

These pieces are based on layers of loops, time stretched motifs and real time recordings, with the addition of lots of effects. They can often sound a bit raw and unpredictable, but it's part of the spirit of these recordings series.
