Deep space ambient full of synthetic textures and dark drones covered with subtle details. Initial Flow tries to picture a personal approach of how the universe and life origins could be. Five long form tracks mixed into a deep and calmed flow forming static and inmersive structures for meditation environments. Last recording did at The Kennel during summer 2002 and based on live improvisations and raw ideas with old equipment.
