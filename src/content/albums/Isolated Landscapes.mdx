Collection of early and unreleased long form material featuring tracks from 1999-2000 era Rituals & In Aeternum private releases with exclusive new soundworlds and new edition. This album also contains the unreleased track Enlightment Cycle from 2001 studio recording at The Kennel. New remastered version includes a new 18 mins bonus track from 1999 Karma album as well as additional treatment on the album.

## Credits

All tracks composed, performed, arranged and produced by Ran Kirlian at The Kennel between 1999 & 2001, remastered at The Blue_Room, 2003.
