Woomh EP containing 4 deeply connected tracks that conforms a sonic portrait of one of those moments when we're feeling lost and blind, like walking inside of a dark forest without a path to follow. While the music of this minialbum is mostly dark ambient, I conceived it as a soundtrack for one of these enlightening experiences that we must feel, and even enjoy, in out lifes.

Dedicated in loving memory to Oöphoi (Gianluigi Gasparetti) 1958 - 2013
