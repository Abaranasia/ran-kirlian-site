This album has been crafted as a soundtrack to synchronize and provide serene soundscapes that echoes the essence of Moodwise, the acclaimed motivational quotes app created by La Terreta Developments. Each track has been meticulously composed to resonate with the uplifting spirit of the application, guiding you through moments of reflection, meditation, and resilience.

The eight full-length tracks contained on this album, spanning over 150 minutes, were created exclusively to capture the essence of the app, but also serve to portray the artist's work throughout his 25-year career. The music contained in this album combines elements from different eras of Ran Kirlian' music; from complex sound worlds and drones to minimalist atmospheres and subtle melodies accompanied by field recordings in order to offer a sanctuary for your soul to find solace, strength and enlightenment for face our daily challenges. In Moodwise we find reminiscences of albums such as Distant Signals, Enlightment Paths or Ending of an Era but also to previous periods such as Continuum, Fade Out and Isolated Landscapes among others.

Just as Moodwise uplifts your spirits with motivational wisdom, allow this music to envelop you in a cocoon of tranquility, empowering you to navigate life's journey with grace and resilience. Embark on a musical voyage that echoes the power of words to elevate your mindset and elevate your soul. Let this music accompany you on your quest for daily inspiration and enlightenment.
