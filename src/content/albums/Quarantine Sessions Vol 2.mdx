The performances included on this album, sessions 2 and 4, were recorded live at The Blue_Room on 19th and 23th March 2020 during Covid-19 home confinement and consist in a total of 11 different pieces (or sound environments) mixed on 2 large tracks. The tracks has not being divided due the continuous flow of the performance. Also, like on the previous volume, the recording has no editions or overdubs and have minimal corrections and treatment beyond mastering in order to preserve the essence of the experience.

Watch the video performances of [session 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O6LCDhmv4c8&t=593s) and [session 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AKmfzdhJc0E&t=761s)

- Minimal eurorack system
- Soma Lyra-8 & Nordlead Nordrack 2x, Access Virus Indigo 1 & 2, Roland JP 8080 and Waldorf Microwave XT
- Audiodamage Quanta and Amazing Noises Reverb on Apple iPad Pro & Lexicon Omega audio interface
