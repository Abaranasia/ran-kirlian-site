This volume contains 2 tracks, each one composed of different pieces, most of them exploring drone and meditative terrains, forming the most relaxing volume of all of them.

The performances included on this album, sessions 3 and 5, were recorded live at The Blue_Room on 20th and 26th March 2020 and, as on the previous ones, the tracks have not being divided and have no editions or overdubs beyond mastering and minimal corrections in order to preserve the essence of the experience and the continuous flow of the performances.

Watch the video performances of [session 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoOfVJeJNqc&t=1286s)

- Minimal eurorack system
- Soma Lyra-8, Access Virus Indigo 1 & 2 and Waldorf Q Rack
- Audiodamage Quanta and Amazing Noises Reverb on Apple iPad Pro & Lexicon Omega audio interface
