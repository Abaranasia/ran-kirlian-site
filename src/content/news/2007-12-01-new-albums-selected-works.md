Brief collection of Ran Kirlian music thru the years from 2000 to 2007. This double album collects tribal-ambient (vol I) and dark and space-ambient sides (vol II), including 4 previously unreleased tracks and 2 rare live versions. You can download **for free** the full albums in zip files, including all tracks in mp3 at **256kbps** and cover art.
