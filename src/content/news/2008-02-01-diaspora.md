**Diaspora**, a real-time improvisation performed and recorded on sunday feb 17 2008 at **The BlueRoom**. The video also includes scenes of sunshines and cloud storms recorded by myself from a window of my home. This is my first attempt to record a video around my music, so excuse me if it looks too amateur.

As it can be seen on the video, this is part of BlueRoom studio. Among the synths there, the ones I play for the track are: Access Indigo Redback, Access Indigo, Waldorf Microwave XT, Roland JP-8080. Tribal groove shoot via computer while recording the session. More information about my gear and more synthesizer facts can be found on my blog [Celiar Structures](http://celiar.blogspot.com/).
