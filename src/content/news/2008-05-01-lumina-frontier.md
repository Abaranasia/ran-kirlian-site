**Lumina Frontier** is, again, a real-time improvisation performed and recorded on may 1 2008 at **The BlueRoom**, done only with a Korg TR61 and no post edition.

All images from NASA found at internet.

Video edition & effects done with Sony Vegas.

More information about my gear and more synthesizer facts can be found on my blog [Celiar Structures](http://celiar.blogspot.com/).

You can watch the video by clicking the play control at the bottom of the image, but if you click on the link below, you will be directed to [Vimeo](http://www.vimeo.com) site, where you can see the same video at bigger resolution and also post comments if you want.
