Another real-time recording is now online with it's own video, this time mixing images from recording session with some infrared captures I did some time ago. Recorded on sunday, june 15 2008 at **The BlueRoom** by Ran Kirlian.

Story behind the video:

I use to like to visit desertic and abandoned places. One evening I was taking pictures in a place where nobody was except me while, suddenly, a strange kid appeared and wanted me to follow him to a place where he said there was something important buried I must discover. After some minutes walking in silent, we arrived to an old ruined school and then, he asked me to cross a door to find his friends but not to look at the right door, because there was the bad teacher room...

Synths:

- Korg MS2000: noise waves

- Oberheim OB12: big space pads

- Alesis Micron: filter pads

- Sounds like voices, bells, storms & other strange noises added on final mix.
