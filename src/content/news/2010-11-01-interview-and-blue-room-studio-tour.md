I've been interviewd recently on [Los Sueños Lúcidos de Alicia](https://xn--sueoslucidosdealicia-56b.blogspot.com/), aradio and podcast spanish program mostly based on interviews of all kind of style based artists. The program plays weekly on [TEA FM](https://web.archive.org/web/20100917062708/http://digitea.es/es/teafm/que_es.php) (98.9 FM, Zaragoza) and has an online player with 64 kbps audio quality that I embed here:

The interview was also available as a 192kbps download (Sueños Lúcidos de Alicia nº35), but that download link no longer exists.

My interview, shared with others, starts on minute 32 and last for 20 or 25 minutes. The audio is in spanish, as well as the complete transcripted text I share on this pdf file: [entrevista para SLA](/entrevista-para-sla.pdf).

Additionally, I've created a video tour thru my home studio, The **Blue_Room**, as a multimedia support for the interview, where I talk about my tools (mostly synthesizers) while I show and play most of them. The audio on this video is also in spanish, but I'm considering to prepare another version with english subtittles.
