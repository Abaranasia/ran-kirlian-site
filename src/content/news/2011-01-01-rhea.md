Improvisation recorded at The Blue_Room on january 1, 2011.

**Tools:**

- Ensoniq Fizmo: soundworld, noises & insects sequence

- HG Fortune Scapeswizard: rumble drones, second bell sequence & sound effects (shoot from Moog Little Phatty)

- Korg Z1: pads

- Access Virus Indigo: pad chords

**Notes:**

Fizmo plays background drones and noises from begining. On min 2:20, while playing Korg Z1 I also play its touchpad with left hard (not seen on video because the left bottom corner of Little Phatty is covering the angle), disturbing a bit the sound. Scapeswizard is driven and shoot from Little Phatty and starts sounding on 3:48 with sounds effets and dronning on 4:40. Last minute of the track is a Scapeswizard solo. Sound recorded included extra reverb from TC Electronic M-One & DAW.

All pictures were taken by myself some time ago, most of them are near IR.
