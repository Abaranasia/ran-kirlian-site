I recently licensed some of my music with **[ad21 Music](http://www.ad21music.com/)**, a record label and online CD & Digital Downloads store featuring ambient music. The label is owned and operated by **[Bruno Sanfilippo](http://www.bruno-sanfilippo.com/)**, [**Max Corbacho**](http://www.maxcorbacho.com/) and now Ran Kirlian, so it is from now, my official label and place to purchase my music. Not all of my work will be available there, as some of my albums are under [Creative Commons license](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/3.0/) and I want them to remain this way. For now, just three of my works are available for purchase there in **FLAC + MP3 file Download** formats including all printable album art and metadata. The albums available at ad21 Music are: [Sleeper](/albums/sleeper/), [Elements in Motion](/albums/elements-in-motion/) and [Fade Out](/albums/fade-out/).
