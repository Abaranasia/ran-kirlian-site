**[ad21 Music](http://www.ad21music.com/)** label wants to celebrate the first year of our online store with a very special gift: a free download sampler with over 1 hour of high quality ambient music by **[Bruno Sanfilippo](http://www.bruno-sanfilippo.com/)**, [**Max Corbacho**](http://www.maxcorbacho.com/) and Ran Kirlian. **Aftermath of a quantum nature** collects music from the best selling albums of our catalogue presented in both flac and mp3 formats with an exclusive booklet, just like any other of our products, but completely free for all our customers and visitors. We think Aftermath of a quantum nature is an excellent reference of the music we like and create here, at ad21music. We hope you like it as much as us!
