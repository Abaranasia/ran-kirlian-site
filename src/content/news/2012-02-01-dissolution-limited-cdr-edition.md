Dissolution is the lastest exploration into dark ambient realms by Ran Kirlian after four years in the making and several sound experiments and project evolutions. The album collecte music in a very organic way, where synth drones melts with environmental noises, acoustic sounds and voices to create soundworlds that drives the listener from the deeps of the earth to the cosmic void. [More info](/albums/dissolution/)

Now, two years after the first edition it has been recorded in CDr format and ready for purchase. It will be presented in a limited edition of 25 units and will soon be ready to purchase from my upcomming record label. If you want a copy, please stay tunned.
