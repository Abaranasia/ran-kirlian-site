**[Altera Orbe](http://www.alteraorbe.com)** is an independent music label owned and managed by the ambient artists **Ran Kirlian** & **Landru**. It is mainly focused on the promotion and diffusion of space, electronic, tribal and dark ambient musical genres. Altera Orbe borns as a digital download division of **[ad21 Music](http://www.ad21music.com/)** label as they will focus exclusively on the release, distribution and mailorder sales of physical products from now on.

In our website (www.alteraorbe.com) you may find an online CD & Digital Download Store supporting the works and projects of the artists that belong to the label: **[Bruno Sanfilippo](http://www.bruno-sanfilippo.com/), [Landru](http://landrumusic.com/), [Max Corbacho](http://www.maxcorbacho.com/), [Ran Kirlian](http://www.rankirlian.com/)** and our partners from [**Relaxed Machinery**](http://relaxedmachinery.com/) record label. Our Digital Download products are offered both in high quality lossless FLAC files and MP3 files including a complete set of the cover artwork in a hi-resolution print ready format, all packaged in a single file for simpler and faster downloading with affordable prices.

In order to show some of the music we are releasing in Antera Orbe we've created a [SoundCloud account](http://soundcloud.com/altera-orbe/sets/altera-orbe-ambient-record-1) with a almost 2 hours of music. You can get informed of our lastest releases by subscribing our newsletter or following us at [Altera Orbe in Facebook](http://www.facebook.com/AlteraOrbe).

As metioned in the previous entry, **[Dissolution](http://www.facebook.com/AlteraOrbe)** can now be purchased in variety of ways: as a **limited CDr edition**, as digital download in **FLAC loseless format** and **MP3/256kbps** format, all three available at [Altera Orbe ambient music label and store](http://www.alteraorbe.com). It can also be download for free at [Jamendo](http://www.jamendo.com/es/album/54702) in MP3/192kbps format. The limited CDr edition is only a 25 units run, so hurry up to get your copy!

We've also re-released my two album compilations from year 2007 named **[Selected Works I](/albums/selected-works-i)** (tribal-ambient music) and **[Selected Works II](/albums/selected-works-ii)** (ethereal and textures). While they will keep available from [Jamendo](http://www.jamendo.com/es/artist/Ran_Kirlian_(2)) in mp3/192kbps as free downloads, the new editions have been remastered, remixed and sound enhanced and also includes booklets with print ready quality. They can be found in FLAC loseless format at **5.00€** each one or in MP3 format at 256kbps on **3.00€**.
