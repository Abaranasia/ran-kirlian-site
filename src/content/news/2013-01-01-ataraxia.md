**Ataraxia** is a Woomh EP of four electronic tracks that are hard to catalogue into any ambient style beyond experimental, yet consistently fit the Woomh concept. It collects music created between 2012 and 2013 with software and hardware synthesizers, samplers and effects, building an alternative sonic palette to the most typical Kirlian textures.
