**Los Bosques Sombrios** is a Woomh EP of four deeply connected tracks that form a sonic portrait of those moments when we feel lost and blind, like walking inside a dark forest without a path to follow. Mostly dark ambient, it was conceived as a soundtrack for one of those enlightening experiences we must feel, and even enjoy, in our lives. Dedicated in loving memory to Oöphoi (Gianluigi Gasparetti, 1958–2013).
