**Distant Signals** is a personal soundtrack for a deep space exploration, both inner and outer, built from pure electronic atmospheres on minimal structures where dark and light combine in a flow of textures and subtle melodies. Its initial recordings were made in live studio sessions during summer 2010; after being remixed, shelved and even abandoned over the following years, the album was finally completed and released, keeping its occasionally out-of-control sound to preserve the essence of the creative process.
