**Many Futures I** opens a series of mini-albums that collect musical and sonic impressions from years of science fiction reading: more than a soundtrack for those stories, a personal vision of the many alternative futures of humankind portrayed by their authors. This first issue includes sonic landscapes inspired by novels and short stories by Ray Bradbury, Arthur C. Clarke and Stanislaw Lem.
