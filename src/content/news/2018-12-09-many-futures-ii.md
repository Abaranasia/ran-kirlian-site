**Many Futures II** is the second mini-album in the series of sonic impressions inspired by years of science fiction reading. This issue includes sonic landscapes inspired by novels and short stories by Theodore Sturgeon, Brian Aldiss, Clifford D. Simak and Robert Silverberg.
