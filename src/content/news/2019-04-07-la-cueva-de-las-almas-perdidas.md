**La Cueva de las Almas Perdidas** (The Cave of the Lost Souls) is a mini-album based exclusively on layers and loops of voices treated with different reverb effects. All drones, sounds and choirs were made with Ran Kirlian's own voice and, occasionally, his son Alan's voice and a rotating corrugated tube. It is dedicated to all the victims during and after Francoism whose bodies were never found.
