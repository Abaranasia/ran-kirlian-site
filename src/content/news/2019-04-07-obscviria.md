**OBSCVIRIA** is a dark-ambient mini-album by Ran Kirlian and Landru that combines dark soundworlds, sequences and rhythms. After reconnecting in 2014, the two artists set out to build something very different from their earlier collaboration Waterfront (2012); the first mix was ready in 2015, and after being set aside several times between other projects, the work was completed during the second half of 2018.
