**The Woomh Files** collects music from the Altera Orbe Woomh series, a number of mini-albums mainly based on experimental, electronic and dark-ambient genres, recorded between 2009 and 2019 and released digitally from 2012 onwards. Besides music from each mini-album, it includes unreleased material and extra information about the making of the recordings and their artworks.
