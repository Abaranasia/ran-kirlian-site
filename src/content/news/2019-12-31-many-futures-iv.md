**Many Futures IV** is the fourth mini-album in the series of sonic impressions inspired by years of science fiction reading. Issue 4 includes sonic landscapes inspired by short stories by Edmond Hamilton, Philip K. Dick, Isaac Asimov and John MacDonald.
