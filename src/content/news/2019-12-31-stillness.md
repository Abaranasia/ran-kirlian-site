**stillness** proposes an isolated and timeless sonic universe that combines and contrasts stasis and dynamism, with slow sequences and melodies flowing and evolving over even slower structures on mostly static drones. The album suggests an alternative approach to the classic concept of environmental drones, applying patterns similar to those used in works such as Ethernautica.
