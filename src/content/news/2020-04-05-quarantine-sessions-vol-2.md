**Quarantine Sessions Vol 2** collects sessions 2 and 4, recorded live at The Blue_Room on 19 and 23 March 2020 during the Covid-19 home confinement. Eleven different pieces are mixed into two long tracks, left undivided to keep the continuous flow of the performance, with no edits or overdubs.
