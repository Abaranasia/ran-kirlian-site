**Quarantine Sessions Vol 3** collects sessions 3 and 5, recorded live at The Blue_Room in March 2020. Its two tracks, each made of different pieces mostly exploring drone and meditative terrains, form the most relaxing volume of the series, again without edits or overdubs beyond mastering and minimal corrections.
