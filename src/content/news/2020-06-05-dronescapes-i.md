**Dronescapes I** collects five long-format pieces born from the exploration of voices and field recordings combined with synthesizers and effects from a drone perspective: an evolving combination of living sounds floating on a suspended acoustic biome. Originally conceived as a 2CD album, it includes two bonus tracks of 50+ minutes each that expand the sonic range of the work.
