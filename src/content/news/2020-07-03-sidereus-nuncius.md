**Sidereus Nuncius** (Sidereal Messenger) is a space ambient album, released by Altera Orbe, that borrows its title from Galileo Galilei's 1610 astronomical treatise. An ode to the vastness of space and to the history of its investigation, it may sound like a natural continuation of Cosmos (2013) and includes the participation of Jaja on "Elliptical Orbit".
