**Primordial Stages** is a dark-ambient album, released by Altera Orbe, that was started more than ten years earlier in parallel to Dissolution and went through a long evolution while remaining faithful to its original idea, inspired by the early stages of life. It sometimes sounds dark and cold and sometimes unpredictable, raw and even noisy, making its listening a unique experience, just like life is.
