**Ethernautica V** is the fifth instance in the series of recordings based on long-format drones and processed raw material, with three medium-length pieces from recording sessions during 2020. They are generated from layers of loops, time-stretched motifs and real-time recordings with lots of effects, and may sound a bit raw and unpredictable, which is part of the spirit of the series.
