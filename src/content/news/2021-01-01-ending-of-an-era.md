**Ending of an Era** is a mini-album composed and recorded during the 2020 Christmas days to shape the feelings left by that year and recapitulate the Covid-19 global pandemic, built from experiments with audio applications recorded on the fly. It is dedicated to all the people lost and found during the Covid-19 pandemic.
