**Ethernautica VI** is the sixth volume in the series of recordings based on long-format drones and processed raw material, with three medium-length pieces from recording sessions between September and December 2021. They are generated from layers of loops, time-stretched motifs and real-time recordings with lots of effects, and were originally created as a source of inspiration and for meditative purposes.
