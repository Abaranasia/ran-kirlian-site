**Ethernautica VII** is the seventh volume in the series of recordings based on long-format drones and processed raw material, with two long pieces from recording sessions between September and December 2022. They are generated from layers of loops, time-stretched motifs and real-time recordings with lots of effects, and may sound a bit raw and unpredictable, which is part of the spirit of the series.
