**Ethernautica VIII** is the eighth volume in the series of recordings based on long-format drones and processed raw material, with three long-form pieces built from improvised recordings that underwent editing and time-stretching between September and December 2023. Based on layers of loops, time-stretched motifs and real-time recordings with lots of effects, they were originally created as a source of inspiration and for meditative purposes.
