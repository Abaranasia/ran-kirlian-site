**Moodwise Soundtrack** was crafted as a soundtrack of serene soundscapes for Moodwise, the motivational quotes app created by La Terreta Developments. Its eight full-length tracks, spanning over 150 minutes, combine elements from different eras of Ran Kirlian's music, from complex sound worlds and drones to minimalist atmospheres and subtle melodies accompanied by field recordings.
