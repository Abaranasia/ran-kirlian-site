**Ethernautica IX** is the ninth volume in the series of recordings based on long-format drones and processed raw material, with three long-form pieces built from improvised recordings that underwent editing and time-stretching between July and December 2024. Recorded and mastered at 24-bit, they may sound a bit raw and unpredictable, which is part of the spirit of the series.
