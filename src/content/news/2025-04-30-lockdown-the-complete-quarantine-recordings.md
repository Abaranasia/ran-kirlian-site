**Lockdown: The Complete Quarantine Recordings** marks the fifth anniversary of the live sessions Ran Kirlian recorded during the 2020 Covid-19 lockdown, originally released on YouTube and Bandcamp as The Quarantine Sessions volumes I, II and III. It compiles eight hours of music, including the original recordings remastered at 24-bit, previously unreleased material recorded at the time, and a brand-new album based on recycled material from those sessions.
