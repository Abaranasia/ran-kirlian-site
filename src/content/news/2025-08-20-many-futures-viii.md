**Many Futures VIII** is the eighth volume of the Many Futures series of mini-albums, which collect Ran Kirlian's musical and sonic impressions from years of reading science fiction. This volume includes sonic landscapes inspired by short stories by Damon Knight, Pamela Sargent and Mildred Clingerman.
