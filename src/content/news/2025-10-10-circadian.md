**Circadian** reunites Spanish ambient artists Ran Kirlian and Landru, thirteen years after their first collaboration and six years after their last, for a dark, mellow sound experience partially recorded live between 2023 and 2024. Built upon synthesizer textures, organic samples and field recordings, the album is a meditation on time, transition and the cyclical nature of existence.
