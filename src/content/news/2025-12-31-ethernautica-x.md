**Ethernautica X** is the tenth volume in the series of recordings based on long-format drones and processed raw material, released as a double CD to celebrate ten years of the series, with over two hours of music across four tracks. Based on improvised recordings, layers of loops, time-stretched motifs and heavily manipulated field recordings, the pieces can sound raw, unpredictable and even aggressive, which is part of the spirit of the series.
