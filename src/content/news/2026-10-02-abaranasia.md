**Abaranasia** collects music recorded between October and December 2003 exclusively with an Ensoniq Fizmo synthesizer, stored and forgotten for years on a hard drive that was later recovered. Mostly improvised, the pieces have been cleaned up, produced and subtly enhanced while preserving their essence, evoking a forgotten world lost in time.
