Ritual of Darkness! So good. Hard to believe these are 'unrelated' tracks...they're all very good, very long, spun out spindling stretches of sound barely rising above a whisper. No harsh peels of clanging or loon calls to disturb the mood. More 'drone' and 'ambient' should be like thus.
